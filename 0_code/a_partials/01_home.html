While at the Federal Reserve Bank of Atlanta I became particularly interested in firm dynamics, innovation, international trade, and corporate finance. These interests are reflected by my published work with Drs. Baslandze and Fang, as well as my other Atlanta Fed co-authors Brent Meyer, Drs. Simon Fuchs, David Wiczer.

I am an incoming Economics PhD student at the University of California, San Diego.
